Dear Clinic Builder,

Tonight, somebody will read three clinic websites before they go to bed. One of them will be yours. They have already decided on the procedure. Now they are deciding on the surgeon.

Two of those websites will lose them in the first screen.

One will get the enquiry.

It is rarely the prettiest one. Pictures get attention. Words, order and one obvious next step get the booking. So before you think about colours and fonts, ask yourself honestly:

  • Does your header say who you help and what to do next, in one glance?
  • Is the tone right for a nervous patient, not a fellow consultant?
  • Do you explain your procedures in plain, easy words?
  • Is booking a consultation obvious, from every page, on a phone?

If any of those made you wince, you are not alone. Most clinic websites are built by generalist agencies who would build a restaurant site the same way. We only build for healthcare, and every word, image and button we place has one job: booking patients.

Sensible questions, straight answers.

The swagger stops here. These are the questions UK doctors and clinic owners ask us about medical website design on strategy sessions, answered the way we answer them.

How much does a medical website cost?
It depends on the number of pages and procedures, the features you need (online booking, finance calculators, multiple locations, eCommerce), and whether you want photography, video and copywriting included. Rather than a menu of packages, you get a written proposal with exact pricing after the free strategy session, once we know what the website has to do.
Can you update our existing website, or do you only build new ones?
Both. Sometimes a website needs a rebuild; often it needs new procedure pages, clearer calls to action and better copy on the pages that already rank. On the strategy session we look at your current site with you and tell you honestly which one you need.
Will a new website hurt our Google rankings?
Not when the move is done properly, and that is a big part of our job. We map every page on your old website, keep and improve the content that already ranks, redirect every old URL to its new home and watch Search Console closely after launch. Done carelessly, a redesign can wipe out years of rankings, which is why SEO is built into our process rather than added afterwards.
How do your guarantees work?
Every website built with our 7-Step Patient Generating Website Formula comes with a guarantee of 10 new consults within the first 45 days after launch, and a 90-day money-back guarantee. The full terms of both are written into your proposal, so you know exactly what is covered before you sign anything.
How do you keep the website compliant?
Because we only work in healthcare, compliance is part of how we write, not a check at the end. Copy is written with GMC guidance on advertising your services, the ASA and CAP Code rules on claims and cosmetic procedures, and GDPR for enquiry forms, cookies and patient data in mind. For our clinics in Australia we work to AHPRA's advertising guidelines in the same way.
Who owns the website?
You do. The website, the content, the photography and the software are yours forever, even if we part ways. No hostage holding.
Do you offer hosting and maintenance?
Yes: best-practice hosting, daily backups, staging environments, unlimited SSL, premium WordPress plugins, a CDN, regular updates and priority email support. We offer it for the websites we build and for websites we haven't built.
Which platform do you build on?
Most of our medical websites are built on WordPress, because it is flexible, fast when it is set up properly, and easy for your team to update. For clinics that sell skincare or medical products online, we also build on Shopify.
Can you also run our SEO, Google Ads and social media?
Yes. Medical SEO, Google Ads, social media, photography and video, and patient funnels are all handled by the same in-house team. Many clients start with the website, then add medical SEO or Google Ads to send it more patients.
Can I see examples of websites you have built?
Yes. The websites further up this page are all ours, and you can scroll through each one in full. The before and after case studies show what changed once they went live.
