Our 6-step Google Ads process.

It is a sequence, not a menu. Nothing is spent until the first two steps are done, because an account you can't measure is an account you can't improve.

01

Check before you spend.

The first fortnight is spent making sure the account can't be suspended and every enquiry can be counted.

  • 1
    Compliance checkYour services, your clinic and the first ad copy checked against Google's healthcare and medicines policies, GMC guidance and ASA rules. When a procedure sits in a grey area, we ask Google before launch, not after a suspension.
  • 2

02

Buy the most valuable traffic first.

Whatever the budget, it goes first to the searches closest to a booked procedure.

Before you book, three things need to be true.

This is not for everybody. Google Ads rewards clinics that can measure and follow up, and punishes clinics that want to switch it on and forget about it. So, honestly:

  • You have a procedure worth advertising, and the capacity to take more of it. Ads amplify demand for something specific. "We do everything" campaigns spend everything.
  • Your team answers the phone and follows up fast. A paid enquiry that waits two days for a call back is money you have already spent. We can build the follow-up system with you, but somebody has to say hello.
  • You can give it three months. Leads come in the first weeks. Cost per patient comes down as the data builds. Month three is usually where the account starts to look very different from month one.

If that is you, the next step is a free 30-minute Google Ads strategy session from our London office. We will open your account, or your competitors' ads if you are not running any, while you watch, and tell you what we would change first.

Sensible questions, straight answers.

The swagger stops here. These are the questions UK clinic owners ask us about Google Ads on strategy sessions, answered the way we answer them.

How quickly will Google Ads bring in patients?
Usually within the first two weeks of launch, once compliance and tracking are in place. That is the point of paid search: there is no waiting for rankings. What takes longer is getting the cost per patient down, which typically improves over the first three months as the account collects real enquiry data.
How much should a clinic spend on Google Ads?
It depends on your procedure, your city and how many patients you can take. Competitive procedures in London cost more per click than the same search in a smaller town. On the strategy session we look at real search volumes and click prices for your procedures and give you a budget range with an expected cost per enquiry, before you commit to anything. Your ad spend is paid directly to Google, so you always see exactly what was spent.
What does your Google Ads management cost?
Our minimum is about £1,500 per channel per month, on top of your ad spend. The management fee depends on the number of campaigns, locations and landing pages involved. You will get a written proposal after the strategy session, not a menu.
Will Google let us advertise our treatments?
Mostly, yes, but healthcare is one of Google's most restricted categories. Prescription medicines, some cosmetic procedures, weight loss, and anything that implies a guaranteed result each come with their own rules, and some need certification first. We check every service and every advert before launch, and when something sits in a grey area we ask Google directly rather than risk a suspended account.
Do you guarantee a number of leads or the top ad position?
No. Nobody honest can promise a fixed number of patients from an auction they don't control. What we do is set benchmarks up front for cost per enquiry and cost per booked patient, measure against them every month, and show you the same screenshots and account figures we show on this page.
We already run Google Ads. Can you audit our account?
Yes, and that is a good use of the free strategy session. We will look at your search terms, structure, tracking and landing pages with you, live. Most clinic accounts we open are paying for searches that will never book, or counting things as conversions that are not patients.
Who owns the ad account?
You do. We work inside an account in your name, billed to your card, so the history, the data and the campaigns stay with your clinic whatever happens. No lock-in on the account itself.
Should we run Google Ads or SEO?
Run both if you can. Ads give you patients next week and stop the day you stop paying. SEO takes months to build and then keeps paying back. Most of our UK clients use Google Ads to fill the diary in the first few months while their medical SEO matures, then rebalance the budget once organic enquiries take over.
