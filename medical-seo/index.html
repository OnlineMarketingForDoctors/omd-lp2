Sensible questions, straight answers.

The swagger stops here. These are the questions UK clinic owners ask us on strategy calls, answered the way we answer them.

Do you guarantee page one rankings?
No. No honest healthcare SEO agency can guarantee a specific position, because Google and the AI models change the rules without asking anyone. What we do is set benchmarks up front, measure against them monthly, and show you the same screenshots we show on this page.
How long until we see results from medical SEO?
Tangible movement usually shows from month two or three after launch: better Maps positions, first page for long-tail terms, more enquiries. The competitive short-tail keywords, "rhinoplasty London" for instance, take longer and are worth more. Year two is where the compounding shows.
What does it cost?
Our minimum is about £1,500 per channel per month. Pricing depends on how competitive your procedure and city are, and how much of the website needs rebuilding first. You will get a written proposal after the strategy call, not a menu.
How long is the commitment?
Twelve months for the first year. SEO that is switched off at month four is money spent on a foundation nobody built on. We set performance projections at the start; if every recommendation is followed and nothing improves, you can ask for a review and early termination.
We get most patients from referrals. Does this still apply?
Yes, and more than you would think. Referred patients research you before they book, and many of them read the AI summary or the Maps reviews first. A strong search presence wins the referral you were already going to get and the one you weren't.
Why not just run Google Ads?
Run both if you can. Ads give you patients next week and stop the day you stop paying. Organic listings still take more clicks than the ads above them, and SEO tends to deliver about twice the return of paid search once it matures. Most of our UK clients use ads to bridge the first few months.
How do you stay compliant with UK rules?
Everything we write is checked against GMC guidance, ASA and CAP rules on medical advertising, and GDPR for anything that touches patient data. We have worked under AHPRA and TGA in Australia and HIPAA in the US for over a decade, so the UK framework is familiar territory.
What is AI SEO, and is it different?
It is the work of getting your clinic recommended inside Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT answers, not just listed in the classic ten blue links. The foundations are the same as good medical SEO. The extra layer is structure and consistency: your clinic described the same way everywhere, with reviews and citations a language model can trust.
