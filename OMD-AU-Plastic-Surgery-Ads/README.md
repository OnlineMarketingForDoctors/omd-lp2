# Australian Plastic & Cosmetic Surgery — Paid Ads Examples

Everything in the OMD portfolio for Australian plastic surgery and cosmetic
surgery clients, across Google Ads and social media advertising.
Extracted from "All OMD Work Examples" (Google Ads, Social Media Ads Results,
Social Media Ad Creatives tabs).

## Folders

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `01 Google Ads` | Search and Maps ad screenshots — ICCM, Lumiere, Shire Aesthetics |
| `02 Meta Ads Results` | Campaign performance screenshots — Dr Queenie Chan, Prophile, White Hill |
| `03 Meta Ads Creatives` | Static ad creatives held as files — Ashbrooke, Shiraz |
| `04 Video Creatives (YouTube)` | Index of 45 video creatives — open `video-creatives.html` |

`index.csv` lists every item with client, description, surgical vs non-surgical,
channel and market.

## Surgical vs non-surgical

Marked per row in `index.csv`. The genuinely surgical examples are:

- ICCM — Gynaecomastia Sydney (Google Ads)
- ICCM — Face Lift Surgery (Google Ads)
- Lumiere — Liposuction Sydney (Google Ads)
- White Hill Clinic — Breast augmentation, two months of Meta results

Everything else is cosmetic / non-surgical (thread lifts, Morpheus8,
anti-wrinkle, laser).

Note on Dr Queenie Chan's Meta results: the sheet tags the September 2026 row
"Marketing for Plastic Surgery Clinics" and it is a plastic surgeon's ad
account, but every campaign visible in the screenshot is a non-surgical
treatment — Morpheus8, Alma Laser, PRP hair, Ignite RF, UltraClear. Useful as
proof of lead volume and cost per lead for a plastic surgery practice; not
proof of advertising a surgical procedure.

## Three clients whose market is unconfirmed

**White Hill Clinic**, **Shiraz Cosmetic** and **Dermal Skin Laundry** appear
only in the social tabs. They have no rows in the SEO or AI Search tabs, so
there are no location keywords to place them in a market. They are included
here and flagged, not assumed Australian — worth confirming before any of
them goes in an Australian pitch.

Ashbrooke Cosmetic Surgery is treated as Australian on the strength of a
single "arm liposuction Sydney" ranking, which is thin evidence.
