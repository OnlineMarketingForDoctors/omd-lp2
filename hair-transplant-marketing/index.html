Sensible questions, straight answers.

The swagger stops here. These are the questions hair transplant surgeons and clinic owners ask us on Discovery Calls, answered the way we answer them.

Do you guarantee page one rankings or a number of patients?
No. No honest hair transplant marketing agency can guarantee a specific ranking, because Google and the AI models change the rules without asking anyone. What we do is set benchmarks up front, measure against them monthly, and show you the same screenshots we show on this page. We aim for a return of at least three times your investment, and we say so in writing.
How long until we see results?
Google and social adverts can book consultations within two to four weeks of launch. SEO usually shows tangible movement from month two or three: better Maps positions, first page for long-tail terms, more enquiries. The competitive short-tail keywords, "hair transplant London" for instance, take longer and are worth more. Most clinics see clear results within four to six months, and year two is where the compounding shows.
How do we compete with clinics in Turkey, or cheaper clinics nearby?
Not on price. Patients who go abroad are buying certainty as much as grafts, so we make your clinic the obvious safe choice: the surgeon on camera, real reviews, honest content about cost, aftercare and what happens if something goes wrong, and follow-up that answers every worry before it becomes a reason to book elsewhere. The sales funnel is built to highlight your unique value, so you compete on quality and trust rather than the lowest quote.
What does it cost?
Pricing depends on which services you need, how competitive your city is, and how much of the website needs rebuilding first. If we run adverts for you, we recommend a minimum ad budget of about £1,500 a month per platform, paid directly to Google or Meta and separate from our fee. The sales funnel and the email follow-up sequence are also available on their own. You will get a written proposal after the Discovery Call, not a menu.
Which services should a hair transplant clinic start with?
Usually three: a website or landing page that converts, Google Ads for consultations this month, and SEO for the compounding growth behind it. The email follow-up and AI response go in at the same time because they cost little and save the leads the other three produce. Social advertising, video, content and PR come in once the foundation is booking.
What exactly is included in the sales funnel?
High-converting landing pages, Google and social ad copy, a keyword list for SEO and Google Ads, content topics for your blog, lead magnets for every stage of the buyer journey, email and SMS templates for follow-up, a complete consultation process and sales training materials. We can implement it for you, or hand it to your own team.
How long does the sales funnel take to set up?
Most clinics have the basic funnel running within two to four weeks. Full implementation, including content and advert setup, usually takes one to three months. Many clinics see better lead quality and conversion rates within the first month of it running properly.
We already have a marketing team. Can they use this?
Yes. Your team can use the funnel as a proven framework, with tested strategies and content they can put straight into their own campaigns. Plenty of our clients run it that way, with us on hand for guidance and technical setup.
Does it work for technician-led clinics as well as doctor-led ones?
Yes. The email sequences come in doctor-led and technician-led versions, and the website, advert and consultation content is written to match how your clinic actually works, within the rules on who can say what.
How do you stay compliant with UK advertising rules?
Everything we write is checked against GMC guidance, the ASA and CAP rules on cosmetic interventions, and GDPR for anything that touches patient data. Before and after images, testimonials, time-limited offers and claims about outcomes all have rules, and we have worked under them, and under AHPRA and HIPAA, for over a decade.
How long is the commitment?
Twelve months for SEO in the first year, because SEO that is switched off at month four is money spent on a foundation nobody built on. Advertising and AI services run month to month after an initial three months. We set performance projections at the start; if every recommendation is followed and nothing improves, you can ask for a review and early termination.
Who will actually work on my account?
A dedicated account manager and between three and seven specialists depending on the channels: SEO, PPC, web, content, social and AI. All in-house, all healthcare only, with UK-based team members for the clinics here. You will know their names by the end of week one.
